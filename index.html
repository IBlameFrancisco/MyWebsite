01 About Me

First-generation college student at MIT studying Computer Science and Applied Mathematics.

I'm Francisco Ahudel Zapata — an MIT Interphase Edge Scholar pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics. My research focus is on AI-driven formal verification, currently working on automated theorem proving at MIT CSAIL. I'm also a core member of the AI@MIT Reading Group, where I engage with current AI/ML research on a weekly basis.

Prior to MIT, I graduated Valedictorian from Loganville High School in Georgia, where I led the Mu Alpha Theta Math Honor Society as President and served on Georgia's State Superintendent Student Advisory Council (4% acceptance rate). I'm fluent in English and Spanish, and I work with Python, Java, JavaScript, and Lean 4.
